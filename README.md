# FinanceApp

**FinanceApp** is a cross-platform application built with **Flutter**, designed for **budgeting**, **personal savings**, and **investment tracking**. It lets you log income and expenses by category, analyze financial trends through interactive charts, and track your investment portfolio with automatic calculation of expected returns.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 💸 Income & Expense Management
- Quick logging of incoming and outgoing financial transactions.
- Selection and organization through **dedicated categories** (e.g., housing, transportation, groceries, leisure, salary, etc.).
- Instant overview of your balance, cash flow, and savings capacity.

### 📊 Statistics & Charts
- Visual breakdown of expense and income distribution by category.
- Visual summaries to compare income, expenses, and savings trends over time.

### 📈 Investments & Return Calculation
- Dedicated ledger to track active investments and allocated capital.
- **Estimated/expected return calculation** to evaluate growth over time and support financial planning.
